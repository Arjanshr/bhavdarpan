1. About Us
Bhav Darpan is a mental health service organization dedicated to providing compassionate, confidential, and accessible mental health support across Nepal. Guided by the philosophy “Reflect. Acknowledge. Heal.”, we help individuals better understand their emotions, accept their experiences, and access professional support in a safe, respectful, and non-judgmental environment.
Mental health challenges in Nepal are often compounded by limited access to services, stigma, and lack of awareness. Bhav Darpan seeks to bridge this gap by offering professional counseling through both physical and virtual platforms, making support available to people from diverse backgrounds. We believe mental health care should focus not only on crisis response but also on early support, prevention, emotional well-being, and personal growth, empowering individuals to heal and lead healthier, more fulfilling lives.
